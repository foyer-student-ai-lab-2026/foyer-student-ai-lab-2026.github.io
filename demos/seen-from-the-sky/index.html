What does a roof say that the contract does not?

Solar panels, a pool, a new annex: visible on the official aerial photos, sometimes missing from the home's contract. A starting point, not a detector: six invented roofs and the shape of the comparison.

Demo by the Foyer team. The roofs and what is "seen" on them are sample values, drawn by the page; the canton shares are fictitious. No real photo, no real home. Seeing something on a roof says nothing for sure: it is a question to ask the owner, never a decision.
