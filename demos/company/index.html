Student AI Lab 2026 · Track 5 · A company and its subscription

Every site, every building, before the quote.

A logistics company asks for a multi-risk quote. The underwriter needs each site, the building that stands on it, what that building risks, and the company's filed accounts. This page does it from open data only, locally, and says not found where the open data stops.

Everything about the company is fictitious: its name, its RCS number, its sites, its accounts (amounts invented, in the exact format of the Centrale des bilans). The building and zone shapes are synthetic, drawn to show the method; the real layers come from the collect scripts. No director, no contact person, no request to any outside service: this page loads nothing from the network.

The role, step by step

The underwriter and the prevention expert follow the same four steps for every company. Each step is a section of this page.

Receive the requestFour sites declared: head office, warehouse, workshop by a river, a depot with no street number. Find each site and its buildingAddress on BD-Adresses, geocoded locally; the 2023 footprint that holds the point. Face the zonesEach building against HQ10, HQ100, extreme flood and flash flood; Seveso neighbours; the nearest fire station. Read the accounts, ask, visitKey figures with their year; a pre-filled questionnaire; the prevention visit, site by site.

Site by site

One row per declared site. The shares are computed in this page from the shapes (zone clipped by the footprint, area over area), the same way as scripts/intersect_sites.py. Building first; the parcel is shown next to it because it often says something else.

In: some part of the footprint lies in the zone. Share: area in the zone over footprint area. Sources: BD-Adresses (ACT, CC0), building footprints 2023 (ACT), PCN parcels (ACT, CC BY 4.0), flood and flash-flood hazard maps (AGE, CC0), Seveso (ITM), CGDIS fire and rescue centres (CC0). Shapes synthetic in this prototype.

Each building against the zones

The page draws the parcel, the buildings and the zones itself, from coordinates in metres (LUREF, EPSG:2169, around the address point). No map tiles, no basemap service: an address never leaves the page.

Three cases where the plot and the building disagree

A parcel can touch a flood zone while its building stands outside, and the other way round. A rule written on the parcel surcharges the wrong site and misses the right one.

The filed accounts

Read from the Centrale des bilans file (STATEC), by RCS number. The page parses the embedded XML itself, with the field-id fallbacks the real files need. Size and assets frame the quote; the book value of buildings is not an insured value.

What the deposit gives, and what it does not

    Three traps of the real file

    1. The server cuts the connection. A file streamed directly stopped at a fifth of 2020 without any error. Download to disk, check the size against resources[0].filesize, count the entries read.
    2. A field id changes with the form version. Take the first present: result 8_ResultEx_2020, 8_ResultEx_2016, 1_ResultEx_2010; debts 1_Dett_2016, or before 2016 the sum of 1_DettNonSub_2010 and 1_DettSub_2010. Turnover 7_Chida_2010 only exists in the complete P&L.
    3. A correction replaces the original. Keep the latest YearRef, then the latest deposit, and the P&L of the same year.
