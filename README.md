# My Risk Compass · Student AI Lab 2026

The public site of the challenge: the presentation, the data catalogue, the fictitious Foyer data
and the Mozaïk general conditions.

1. `index.html`: the challenge.
2. `pitch.html`: the presentation. Click "▶ Present" (or press F) for full screen; the four demos run on their own.
3. `data.html`: the public data to use, with links, producers and licences, and the fictitious Foyer data.
4. `data/`: the fictitious Foyer data (a smoothed 500 m grid, communes, cantons, road segments), the commune boundaries, two heatmaps and the data dictionary.
5. `docs/`: the Mozaïk general conditions.

Everything Foyer provides here is fictitious: no policy, no claim, no personal data.
