Fictitious Foyer data

What an insurer knows internally, invented and aggregated at the grain of the public data: a smoothed 500 m grid, communes, cantons, road segments. No address, no household, no policy, no claim record, no personal data. Cross it with the public data by coordinates, commune or canton.

grid_500m.csv

A smoothed 500 m grid over the country: insured homes, water damage, storm and hail, burglary indices, cellars, the natural catastrophes option. A heatmap under any address.

homes_by_commune.csv

The same indicators for each commune, with communes.geojson, the official boundaries to draw and join.

road_segments.csv

The main roads in 1 km segments: a motor claim index, and the share of claims with wildlife, on ice, rear-end in traffic, at night.

homes_by_canton.csv

Insured homes, flats, owners, construction year, cellars, solar panels, alarms, leak detectors, non-return valves.

mozaik_options_by_canton.csv

Share of homes with each Mozaïk option: natural catastrophes, theft, valuables, eco energies…

vehicles_by_canton.csv

Insured vehicles, SUVs, electric or hybrid, age, parked outside, kilometres per year.

commutes_between_cantons.csv

Commuters between cantons, distance, leaving before 7:00, share on forest roads.

Data dictionary · generer.py, which regenerates and checks the files.

Insurance covers

The Mozaïk general conditions: what the base covers, what each option adds, and what is excluded.
